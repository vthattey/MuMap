Reflection Session — mock

How a private-write / synchronized-reveal "Reflection Session" lives inside the existing Retro template (Start / Stop / Continue), mirroring the existing Vote Session header pattern for its owner-only start/end control. Four screens below: the owner's board mid-session, a collaborator's board at the same moment, the live reveal, and the board with no session running for comparison.

Mock-only scaffolding — not part of the app
  • Everything inside a rounded "browser" frame below is a faithful reproduction of real MuMap chrome. Amber dashed boxes and labels (like this one) are mock scaffolding only.
  • Screens 1 & 2 are live and synced — they're the same map. Click "Start reflection session" or "End reflection session" in Screen 1 and watch Screen 2 react at the same moment, the way realtime broadcast actually would.
  • Sample participants: Jordan Lee (map owner, starts/ends the session), Priya Shah and Sam Okafor (collaborators).

Viewing as Jordan Lee, the map owner. Jordan's own cards are always fully visible and editable. Priya's and Sam's cards render as lean lock placeholders until Jordan ends the session — Jordan gets no early peek either, per the plan's "even the owner is blind" rule.
